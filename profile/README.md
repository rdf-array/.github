**rdf-array** is RDF with one more kind of term, the **sequence**: `(1 2 3)` in Turtle is one term
holding three literals in order, and `()` is the empty sequence. These repositories teach RDF
implementations the term, one stack per language, with the test suites they share.

- **Java:** [jena](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#jena) — Turtle-array, SPARQL-array, ShEx-array, SHACL-array
- **JavaScript:** [shex.js](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#shexjs) · [comunica](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#comunica) · [jsonld.js](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#jsonldjs)
- **Python:** [PyShEx](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#pyshex), with rdflib
- **Rust:** [oxigraph](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#oxigraph) · [rudof](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#rudof)
- **Test suites:** [the W3C suite forks and their `*-contrib` siblings](https://rdf-array.github.io/rdf-array/repos.html#test-suites)
